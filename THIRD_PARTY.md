# Third-party sources

Setup downloads source material from:

- video-shotcraft: https://github.com/Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft
- Remotion Agent Skills: https://github.com/remotion-dev/skills
- Remotion packages: https://www.npmjs.com/package/remotion

Third-party files remain under their own licenses/terms. `SETUP.bat` preserves the copied repository files and records source commit SHAs in `skills-lock.json`.

The full video-shotcraft checkout is local under `.vendor/video-shotcraft` and is intentionally Git-ignored; `skills-lock.json` records the exact commit.
